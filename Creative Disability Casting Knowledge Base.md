# Part 1: Conceptual Foundations

## Central Creative Thesis

The principal purpose of storytelling is to encourage audiences to understand one another and broaden their understanding of what it means to be human.

Disabled people have existed in every culture, nation, profession, social class and historical period. Disability therefore represents not only a subject matter but also a source of creative opportunity.

Authentic disability inclusion expands the available range of characters, relationships, perspectives, embodiments, communication styles and storytelling possibilities.

## Core Concepts

### Authentic Casting

Casting a performer whose lived experience has a meaningful relationship to the disability represented within the role.

### Incidental Portrayal

A disabled performer playing a character whose disability is present but is not the principal narrative purpose of the role.

### Condition Transposition

Adapting disability characteristics to align with the performer while preserving the narrative and social meaning of the role.

### Creative Disability Consultancy

A specialist advisory function supporting story, character, portrayal, casting and audience meaning.
